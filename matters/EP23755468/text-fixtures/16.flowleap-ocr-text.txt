2 Clarity

2.1 Claim 1

2.1.1 Claim defines in lines 7-8 a second thermal displacement relief layer. However, the location of said second thermal displacement relief layer is not defined. In fact, it is not mentioned any more in claim 1. As its location is not defined, its technical function is not clear (Art. 84 EPC) and the scope of the claimed scale arrangement is ambiguous and therefore not clear (Art. 84 EPC).
2.1.2 In figures 2 and 3, the second thermal displacement relief layer (208, 308) is located below the intermediate member (206, 306). The first thermal displacement relief layer is located above the intermediate member.
2.1.3 This has to be clarified in claim 1 in order to establish a clear scope of protection in the sense of Art. 84 EPC.
2.2 Claim 5

Merely reference signs may be put in parenthesis (R. 43(7) EPC). Hence, the expression "("FeNi")" has to be reformulated as "i.e. FeNi".

2.3 Claim 9

Claim 9 defines as "claimed in 8". Presumably "as claimed in claim 8" is meant.

2.4 Claims 13-15

2.4.1 Claims 13 corresponds to originally filed claims 17-18 and claim 14 corresponds to originally filed claim 19, which were identified in the international preliminary report on patentability, i.e. IPRP, dated 24.02.2025 under point 1.4.5 to belong to a fourth invention.
2.4.2 Claim 15 corresponds to originally filed claim 20, which was identified in said IPRP under point 1.4.3 to belong to a second invention.
2.4.3 The subject matter of claims 13-15 is incompatible with the scale arrangement defined in claim 1, because the subject matter of claim 13-15 relies on the fact that there is no second thermal displacement relief layer between the intermediate layer and the substrate. This, however, is at the core of the present invention.
2.4.4 In addition, claim 14 defines that "there is no adhesive and/or thermal displacement relief layer between said intermediate member and the substrate", whereas claim 12, on which claim 14 depends, defines that the "thermal displacement relief layer adheres an intermediate member of the thermal displacement relief structure to the substrate". This is contradictory and therefore not clear (Art. 84 EPC).
